What SAMPLE is

SAMPLE is the public demo of that desk. The book is labeled SAMPLE. It is an illustrative journal — Alex Runner, stops held, size leaked — so a stranger can score one trade before creating an account. It is not a customer testimonial and not live assets under management. Nothing in the SAMPLE book is your P&L.

The first screen says they get paid when you skip the stop. Score a trade and you see the slip on that one fill. Saving the score asks for email, then a weekly discipline report that compares the undisciplined result with the clean one. Cold traffic belongs on this desk, not on a sign-in form.
