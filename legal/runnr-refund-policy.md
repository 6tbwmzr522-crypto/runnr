# Refund Policy

**Runnr — Thin Ice Digital Ltd**
Last updated: August 2026

---

## Subscription Model

Runnr operates on a recurring subscription basis — monthly (€19/month) or annual (€190/year). Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

---

## Cancellation

You can cancel your Runnr subscription at any time from the billing portal in your account settings. There are no cancellation fees or penalties.

- **Monthly subscribers:** Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current monthly billing period. You retain full access until then.
- **Annual subscribers:** Cancellation takes effect at the end of the annual period. No partial refunds are issued for the unused portion of an annual term, except as noted below.

---

## Refunds

**General policy:** Runnr subscription fees are non-refundable once a billing period has commenced, as access to the full product is granted immediately upon payment.

**14-day new subscriber exception:** If you are a first-time annual subscriber and cancel within 14 days of your initial purchase without having used the paid features (Coach, broker sync, alerts), you may request a full refund by emailing info@thinicedigital.com. We will process eligible refunds within 5–10 business days.

**Billing errors:** If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, contact us at info@thinicedigital.com within 14 days of the charge. We will investigate promptly and issue a correction where appropriate.

---

## How to Cancel

1. Open Runnr and go to **Settings**
2. Tap **Manage billing**
3. Follow the Stripe billing portal to cancel your subscription

Alternatively, email info@thinicedigital.com and we will process your cancellation manually.
